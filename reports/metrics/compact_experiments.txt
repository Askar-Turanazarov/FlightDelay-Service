COMPACT MODEL EXPERIMENTS (selection of the variant for the public demo)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data, split, features and all CatBoost parameters as in train_final.py (2.1 M rows, 80/20 stratified,
GPU, depth 7, l2 12, scale_pos_weight 4.2, Bernoulli 0.8, seed 42). Only the settings below differ.
ctr = max_ctr_complexity (how many categorical features CatBoost combines in one CTR table).
RAM = resident memory of a Python process after joblib.load of the pkl / peak during loading (Windows, py 3.10).
Precision is reported at the threshold that gives recall 0.82, to compare with the full model (0.306 @ 0.37).

Decision: E (ctr<=3) keeps 0.779 AUC (-0.007) and 0.30 precision at recall 0.82 while fitting the 1 GB free tier
of Streamlit Community Cloud. ctr<=2 and ctr<=1 lose 0.03-0.05 AUC: high-order category combinations carry signal.
Streamlit Community Cloud limits: 1 GB RAM guaranteed, 2.7 GB hard limit; the full model is killed at start-up.
Final model trained by train_compact.py (same settings as E): pkl 114.2 MB, cbm 325.6 MB, ROC-AUC 0.7786 -> compact_model_results.txt
